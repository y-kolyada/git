# Backlog

What was deliberately left out, and why it is not being done now.

## How to write a line

```markdown
- **What.** Why it is needed. Why not now.
```

Three fields and not one more. **"Why not now" matters most of the two others:** it is what
separates a decision from forgetfulness. A line without it comes back as a reproach six weeks
later, when nobody remembers whether it was weighed or dropped.

## Lines

- **The fifteen section titles and the glossary.** The navigator is the site's map, and
  `Раздел 1` is not a map. Not now: the plan of sections is the Architect's to approve, and
  fifteen names invented in passing would be rewritten with the first real one.
- **English, Ukrainian and German.** The series promises four languages, and a stub that points
  at the Russian page is honest but not read. Not now: a fact corrected after translation has
  to be corrected in four places, so the Russian original is accepted first.
- **The examples pages (`ru/10.html` - `ru/12.html`) and their runner.** Nothing on this site
  is believed until it runs, and pages 10-12 are where the runnable blocks live. Not now: the
  per-site runner is one of the two scripts the seed does not stamp (`notes-1.md` §1), and
  there is no example text yet for it to run.
- **A question bank of fifty questions per level.** The site calls itself an assessment in the
  header slogan, and an assessment without questions is a claim. Not now: questions are written
  against finished topics, not against skeletons.
